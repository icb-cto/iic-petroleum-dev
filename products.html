How We Trade

A transparent process,
start to finish.

Clear documentation and independent verification at every step protect both buyer and seller.

  1. Enquiry & KYC

    Share your product, volume, destination and delivery terms. We complete know-your-customer checks on every counterparty.

  2. Offer & contract

    We issue a formal offer, then agree the sale and purchase contract, Incoterms and payment instrument.

  3. Inspection & loading

    An agreed independent inspector certifies quality and quantity before the cargo is released.

  4. Delivery & documents

    The cargo is delivered with a full document set: Bill of Lading and certificates of quality, quantity and origin.
